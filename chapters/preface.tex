\section*{A Note on Feynman Diagrams}
The colors on momentum arrows in Feynman diagrams serve as a visual aid, but do not carry any information that is not otherwise apparent from the diagram. For anyone curious, the momenta are colored \textcolor{PineGreen}{green} for on-shell particles, \textcolor{BrickRed}{red} for off-shell particles, \textcolor{Purple}{purple} for particles in loops and \textcolor{NavyBlue}{blue} for Feynman rules.
